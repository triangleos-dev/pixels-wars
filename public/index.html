Rules
One pixel per player every minute. One account per player. New accounts can create a faction right away but can only join one after 1 hour. New pixels must touch your faction's land. You can capture enemy pixels. If a faction's capital (◆) is captured, the whole faction falls: its land becomes neutral and its members are left without a faction. A new faction's capital is shielded for its first hour.
